How it Works

Step 1: Initialization

Grant webcam access. The system will immediately load the AI model and start mapping your facial landmarks.

Step 2: EAR Analysis

Our algorithm continuously calculates your Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) to monitor blink patterns and eye closure.

Step 3: Action Trigger

If your EAR drops below the safe threshold for a prolonged time, a loud alarm fires to keep you focused.
